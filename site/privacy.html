Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What this app is

Social Autopilot is a personal tool. Its owner uses it to publish their own posts to their own accounts on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit and other platforms. Nobody else signs in to it.

What data it uses

The app only stores the access tokens the owner grants when connecting their own accounts. It uses them only to publish the owner's content and to check that the connections still work. Tokens are stored encrypted, as secrets in a private configuration.

The app doesn't collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone else. It doesn't read followers' data, messages or other people's content, and it uses no tracking or analytics.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's settings, which makes the stored tokens useless. To ask a question or request deletion, open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
