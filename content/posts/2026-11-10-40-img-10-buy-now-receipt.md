One tap. Six handoffs.

Your phone talks to the shop. The shop checks price and stock. A payment gateway encrypts your card details. The card network finds your bank. Your bank says yes or no. The shop saves the order and emails you.

When a checkout breaks, it broke at one of these steps. That's where you start looking.
