9 September 1947. Operators testing the Harvard Mark II found a moth trapped in Relay 70, Panel F.

They taped it into the logbook and wrote: "First actual case of bug being found."

"Actual" is the joke: this bug was a literal insect.

The useful part hasn't changed: when software misbehaves, the cause is usually small, specific and findable.
