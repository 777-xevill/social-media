AI doesn't see "strawberry" the way you do.

Before a language model reads your text, it splits it into tokens: whole words, parts of words, punctuation. We checked: OpenAI's cl100k_base tokenizer cuts strawberry into str, aw and berry. A newer one, o200k_base, cuts it st, raw, berry.

It's part of why chatbots have stumbled on "how many r's are in strawberry?" Not the whole reason, but a good place to start.
