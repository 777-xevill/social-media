AI can be wrong in the same calm voice it uses when it's right.

OpenAI's 2025 paper "Why language models hallucinate" says part of the cause is how models get graded: a guess can score points, "I don't know" scores nothing.

So use it. Just check anything that would cost you if it were wrong.
