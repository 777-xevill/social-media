Not everything should be automated. Five questions decide it:

Does it happen every week? Are the steps the same every time? Is the information already digital? Could it run without a judgement call? Is a mistake cheap to fix?

Five yeses: automate it. Anything else has a smaller, cheaper first step.

Save this, and try it on the task you hate most. This is our own rule of thumb, not a law.
