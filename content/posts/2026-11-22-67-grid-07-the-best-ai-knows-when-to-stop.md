An AI that answers everything is a risk. An AI that knows when to hand over is useful.

Let it answer price, stock, hours, delivery and booking. When it isn't sure, or the customer wants a refund, a person takes over with the whole chat attached.

That's how we design assistants like ReplyDesk.
