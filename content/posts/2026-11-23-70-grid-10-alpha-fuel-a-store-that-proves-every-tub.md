Supplements in Bangladesh have a trust problem: refilled tubs and copied labels.

So Alpha Fuel's store puts the proof up front: foil-seal checks, batch codes and a stock docket you can read before you pay. Orders go through WhatsApp, cash on delivery, bKash and Nagad.

Live at alphafuelbd.com.
