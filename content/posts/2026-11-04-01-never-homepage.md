3 things I'd never put on a client's homepage, as a web designer.

1. A slider. Nobody waits for slide three, so your best message stays hidden.
2. Stock photos of people shaking hands. Show your real work instead.
3. A contact form as the only way to reach you. Put your phone number at the top, one tap away.

Which one is on your site right now?
