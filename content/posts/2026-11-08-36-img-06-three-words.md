Automation, chatbot, AI agent. Same customer question, three different jobs.

Automation follows fixed steps: order ships, tracking email goes out.

A chatbot answers when asked, using what it's been given.

An agent works toward a goal: finds the order, spots the delay, drafts a reply and asks a person before sending.

Save this for the next time someone says "we should add AI."
