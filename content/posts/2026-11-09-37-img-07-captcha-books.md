Remember typing two wobbly words to prove you were human?

You were also doing a job.

reCAPTCHA showed people scanned words that OCR software couldn't read. Their answers helped digitise books and newspaper archives, including The New York Times. The method was published in Science in 2008.

The clever part: it got useful work out of a step people already had to do. Every business has a step like that.
