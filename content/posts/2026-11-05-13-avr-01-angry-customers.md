We gave one free AI model 10 angry messages for a made-up pizza shop, and no rules.

In 7 of the 10 replies, it offered a refund, credit, remake or replacement. Nobody told it that it could. One reply even says "I've issued a full refund".

The test: Gemini 3.5 Flash, one run, 5 Nov 2026. The replies on screen are real. That's one test, not a study. Tony and the voices are made up.
